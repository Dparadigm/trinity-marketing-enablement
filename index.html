How marketing runs the week with Trinity CMO + Copywriter

A practical guide for Growth and content execution: what to open, when to use each agent, how to ship, and how to prove the work moved the funnel.

1. Why this matters

Marketing already gets a steady stream of intelligence and briefs from Trinity. The gap is not “more reports.” It is a clear operating habit:

  • Pick the work that matters this week.
  • Route writing through Copywriter only after a brief is accepted.
  • Stamp traffic so HubSpot can show brief-assisted pipeline.
  • Close the loop on publish and outcomes — not just “brief finished.”
Operating principle: CMO decides what matters. Copywriter drafts the assets. Marketing accepts, stamps UTM, ships, and proves the outcome.

2. What each tool does

ToolUse it forDo not use it for
CMO Diagnosing spend, CPA, and MQL gaps; force-ranking the week; Daily Pulse and the Monday pack culminating in the Synthesis Brief; handing human-gated briefs into the writing lane Final copy, live publishing, brand/compliance final say, or changing ad accounts unattended
Copywriter Turning an accepted brief into drafts for human review before anything goes live Choosing strategy, picking THE ONE THING, or publishing without human approval

Other Trinity agents (for example social/video) are outside this enablement unless leadership expands scope. Treat Drive materials that still say “Ruby” against copy briefs as legacy naming until clarified — marketing writing authority for this scorecard is Copywriter.

3. The weekly rhythm

What shows up in the inbox

  • Daily Pulse on weekdays — a short health check, not a full replan.
  • Monday pack — Ads Audit, Competitor, Trends, SEO, Marketing Summary, SDR, Sales, Strategic Analysis, and the Synthesis Brief (the force-ranked work plan).
  • Delivery is email plus a Drive Doc link (from reports@paradigmlife.net into the marketing distribution list). Slack is for working the items, not for replacing the brief.

How the week should run

WhenOpen firstMarketing does
Monday · 30–45 min Synthesis Brief Accept / Defer / Kill each ranked item. Lock THE ONE THING. Assign owners and due days. Require a testable outcome on every Accept.
Mid-week · ~15 min Whatever the brief pointed to; Copywriter drafts when writing is needed Execute Accepted items. Use CMO when spend, CPA/MQL diagnosis, or a new brief is needed. Human-gate every draft before publish.
Friday · ~15 min That week’s Accept log For each Accept: shipped? Did the metric move? Carry forward with a reason, or Kill.
Known failure mode: brief marked done while the page or asset is still not live. “Done” means published (or intentionally killed) — not drafted only.

4. Accept / Defer / Kill

DecisionMeaningRequired
AcceptExecute in this windowOwner + due day + testable outcome + UTM slug if the work can create or re-enter an MQL
DeferValid, not this weekWhy + earliest reopen week
KillWrong, stale, or un-actionableOne-line why (feeds “improve the brief machine”)
  1. No Accept without a projected outcome. Activity is not done.
  2. THE ONE THING should be completable by Wednesday; if not, Friday notes why.
  3. Carry-overs older than two weeks get Killed or re-scoped — no infinite roll.
  4. If two reports disagree, treat it as a judgment call: do not scale spend until resolved.
  5. If it is not in the brief and not an emergency, it waits until next Monday.

5. UTM on brief-driven work

HubSpot cannot tag a contact to a Trinity brief ID today. UTMs are the bridge so brief-driven pushes can be measured against MQLs and speed-to-lead.

utm_campaign=trinity_brief_<slug>   ← required on brief-driven pushes
utm_source=meta | google | linkedin | email | website | youtube | other
utm_medium=paid | organic | email | nurture | enablement | ppc | paid_social
utm_content=<creative-or-variant>     ← optional, no spaces
  • Build the slug from the accepted brief title (kebab-case). Keep it stable for the life of that Accept.
  • Use the same campaign on every creative for that brief.
  • Blank UTM counts in the overall book only — it will not show up as brief-assisted.

Example (Meta paid)

?utm_source=meta&utm_medium=paid&utm_campaign=trinity_brief_infinite-banking-investing&utm_content=lead_investing-interest

6. How impact is measured

The scorecard weights outcomes and brief execution over raw “opened a report.” Usage without movement in the funnel is a coaching signal, not a win.

LensWeight (guide)What marketing should care about
Enablement / habit ~20% Can the team reach CMO and Copywriter? Was Synthesis opened? Did drafts reach human review?
Synthesis execution ~30% Items claimed · time to first draft · time to published · reopen rate
Funnel outcomes ~50% Brief-assisted MQLs · High vs Standard intent · first-CALL rate · hours to CALL · SLA · SDR coverage

Outcome ladder

L1 spend efficiency → L2 traffic / landing conversion → L3 MQL quality → L4 speed and SLA → L5 SQL → L6 revenue. Intermediate steps matter; vanity opens do not.

8. Q4 rock alignment

The monthly content rock (ICP → CMO topic cards → Zoom polls → lock → publish → measure) is the north star for Q4. Weekly CMO and Copywriter usage should serve that rock, not compete with it for bandwidth — especially through content-lock weeks.
