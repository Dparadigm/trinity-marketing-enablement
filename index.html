How marketing runs the week with Trinity CMO + Copywriter

A practical guide for Growth and content execution: what to open, what to Accept, what to ship, and how to see whether Synthesis-driven work moved the funnel.

Operating board: Weekly scorecard — Accepts tied to live site URLs and funnel calls.

1. Why this matters

Trinity already surfaces intelligence and force-ranked work. The operating gap is turning that into shipped marketing and knowing whether it worked.

  • Accept the work that matters this week.
  • Route writing through Copywriter only after a brief is Accepted.
  • Ship with human gate — draft is not done.
  • See funnel movement on that work, then keep or change course.
Operating principle: CMO decides what matters. Copywriter drafts the assets. Marketing accepts, ships, and judges impact from funnel outcomes — not from opening reports.

3. Weekly rhythm

What shows up

  • Daily Pulse on weekdays — a short health check, not a full replan.
  • Monday pack — Ads Audit, Competitor, Trends, SEO, Marketing Summary, SDR, Sales, Strategic Analysis, and the Synthesis Brief (the force-ranked work plan).
  • Delivery is email plus a Drive Doc link (from reports@paradigmlife.net into the marketing distribution list). Slack is for working the items, not for replacing the brief.

How the week should run

WhenOpen firstMarketing does
Monday · 30–45 min Synthesis Brief Accept / Defer / Kill each ranked item. Lock THE ONE THING. Assign owners and due days. Require a testable funnel outcome on every Accept.
Mid-week · ~15 min Whatever the brief pointed to; Copywriter drafts when writing is needed Execute Accepted items. Use CMO when spend, CPA/MQL diagnosis, or a new brief is needed. Human-gate every draft before publish.
Friday · ~15 min That week’s Accept log + outcome read For each Accept: shipped? Did traffic, clicks, CTR, leads, or engagement move? Keep, change, or Kill — and feed that back into next week’s Synthesis.
Known failure mode: brief marked done while the page or asset is still not live. “Done” means published (or intentionally killed) — not drafted only.

4. Accept / Defer / Kill

DecisionMeaningRequired
AcceptExecute in this windowOwner + due day + testable funnel outcome (what should move: traffic, clicks, CTR, leads/MQLs, engagement)
DeferValid, not this weekWhy + earliest reopen week
KillWrong, stale, or un-actionableOne-line why (feeds “improve the brief machine”)
  1. No Accept without a projected funnel outcome. Activity is not done.
  2. THE ONE THING should be completable by Wednesday; if not, Friday notes why.
  3. Carry-overs older than two weeks get Killed or re-scoped — no infinite roll.
  4. If two reports disagree, treat it as a judgment call: do not scale spend until resolved.
  5. If it is not in the brief and not an emergency, it waits until next Monday.

6. How impact is measured

Impact is not “opened Synthesis” or “used the tools.” Impact is whether Accepted brief work that shipped moved marketing funnel outcomes — and whether the team can see that clearly enough to keep going or change course with AI.

The chain we need visibility on

  1. Surfaced — What Synthesis recommended this week (and what marketing Accepted).
  2. Shipped — What went live from those Accepts (page, ad, email, creative).
  3. Funnel movement — Traffic, clicks, click-through rate, leads/MQLs, and engagement tied to that work.
  4. Call — Making impact → continue. Not making impact → change the brief, the offer, the creative, or Kill — and work the change with AI for the next cycle.

What counts (funnel outcomes)

OutcomeWhy it matters for Synthesis execution
Traffic to the Accepted destinationDid the push get seen?
Clicks and CTRDid the creative/message earn engagement?
Leads / MQLs (and intent split when available)Did engagement become pipeline input?
Downstream speed (first CALL, SLA) when the Accept aimed thereDid marketing handoff convert into sales motion?
Scorecard stance: Funnel outcomes from brief execution are the weight. Enablement and habit are hygiene — useful to notice if the machine is stalled, not a grade that substitutes for leads, traffic, clicks, or engagement.

What marketing should be able to answer every Friday

From Synthesis this week

What did we Accept? What shipped? What is still draft-only?

On the funnel

For those ships: traffic, clicks/CTR, leads/MQLs, engagement — up, flat, or down vs the outcome we named on Accept?

If the answer is flat or down, that is not a failure of “using Trinity.” It is a signal to change the recommended work or the execution — and bring that change back into next Monday’s Synthesis.

Attribution note (UTM): HubSpot cannot tag a contact to a Trinity brief ID today. To connect funnel outcomes to a specific Accept, stamp brief-driven pushes with a stable campaign so traffic, clicks, and MQLs can be read as brief-assisted — not mixed into the overall book. 
utm_campaign=trinity_brief_<slug>   ← required on brief-driven pushes
utm_source=meta | google | linkedin | email | website | youtube | other
utm_medium=paid | organic | email | nurture | enablement | ppc | paid_social
utm_content=<creative-or-variant>     ← optional, no spaces

Slug = kebab from the Accepted brief title; keep it stable for the life of that Accept. Same campaign on every creative for that brief. Blank UTM still counts in the overall book — it will not show as brief-assisted. Example (Meta paid):

?utm_source=meta&utm_medium=paid&utm_campaign=trinity_brief_infinite-banking-investing&utm_content=lead_investing-interest

7. Q4 rock alignment

The monthly content rock (ICP → CMO topic cards → Zoom polls → lock → publish → measure) is the north star for Q4. Weekly CMO and Copywriter usage should serve that rock, not compete with it for bandwidth — especially through content-lock weeks.
