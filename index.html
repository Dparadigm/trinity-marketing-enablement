5. Enablement 1-pager (what they’d get)

Audience eventually: Rachel Glaittli (Growth & Marketing Strategy) · John Keever (Copywriter / paid-ops execution). Status: draft for you first.

One sentence: CMO decides what matters this week. Copywriter drafts the assets. Humans Accept, stamp UTM, ship, and prove the outcome.

Hard rules

  1. No Accept without a projected, testable outcome (L1–L6). Activity ≠ done.
  2. THE ONE THING completable by Wednesday; if not, Friday notes why.
  3. Carry-overs older than 2 weeks → Kill or re-scope.
  4. If two reports disagree = JUDGMENT-CALL — don’t scale spend until resolved.
  5. If it isn’t in the brief and isn’t an emergency, it waits until next Monday.

Q4 rock alignment

Rachel’s rock (ICP → CMO topic cards → Zoom polls → lock → Riverside/podcast publish → measure with John) is the north star. Weekly CMO/Copywriter usage should serve that rock, not compete with it.

6. UTM convention (HubSpot bridge)

Problem: HubSpot can’t tag contacts to a Trinity brief ID today.
Fix (no schema change): stamp every brief-driven push with a stable campaign.

utm_campaign=trinity_brief_<slug>   ← required
utm_source=meta | google | linkedin | email | website | youtube | other
utm_medium=paid | organic | email | nurture | enablement | ppc | paid_social
utm_content=<creative-or-variant>     ← optional, no spaces
  • Slug = kebab from brief title; stable for the life of that Accept; don’t rename mid-flight.
  • Same campaign on every creative for one brief.
  • Blank UTM = overall book only — not brief-assisted layer.
  • Pulse attributes weekly on prefix trinity_brief_.
  • First Monday fill waits until UTMs are actually stamped after you green-light share.

Example (Meta paid)

?utm_source=meta&utm_medium=paid&utm_campaign=trinity_brief_infinite-banking-investing&utm_content=lead_investing-interest
